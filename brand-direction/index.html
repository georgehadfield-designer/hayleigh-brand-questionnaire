Brand identity exploration · October 2026

Finding your way back to you.

An initial creative direction for Hayleigh Monique Coaching. Warm, honest and quietly confident.

Prepared as a starting point for discussion, not a finished identity.

01 / The opportunity

Successful on paper. Searching for something more.

For women who have achieved a lot, but feel disconnected from what they really want.

The brand should make someone feel immediately understood — without promising an overnight transformation. This is about reflection, clarity and the confidence to choose a next chapter that feels genuinely theirs.

“I had built a life that looked good to everyone else, but wasn't sure it felt right for me.”
02 / Audience

Capable. Thoughtful. Established.

What she feels

Professionally successful but unsure what comes next. Pulled between her own ambitions and other people's expectations.

What she needs

Space to think, a non-judgemental sounding board and practical support to make considered decisions.

03 / Brand personality

Calm, but motivating.

WarmRelatableHonestModernUpliftingReassuring

Aspirational without being intimidating. Conversational without feeling casual or unconsidered.

Avoid: corporate, clinical, overly spiritual, generic and overly bold visual language.

04 / Creative directions

Two ways to bring the story to life.

Direction A

Quiet Confidence

Warm ivory, taupe, charcoal and elegant editorial typography. Spacious layouts, natural photography and understated detail.

A little more you.

Feels: grounded, considered, sophisticated, approachable.

Direction B

The Next Chapter

Soft rose, muted plum, editorial contrast and expressive typographic moments. A more optimistic, contemporary energy.

What's next is yours.

Feels: fresh, confident, personal, forward-looking.

06 / Messaging

Speak to the feeling, not a formula.

Option one
You've built a successful life. But does it feel like yours?
Option two
Find your way back to you.
Option three
Doing well. Feeling lost. Sound familiar?
07 / Recommendation

Quiet Confidence, with an editorial edge.

Start with the warmth and restraint of Direction A, then borrow some of the typographic expression of Direction B.

The goal is a personal brand that feels like a thoughtful conversation with someone who understands — not another motivational coaching business.
