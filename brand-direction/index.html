Brand strategy exploration · October 2026

Finding your way back to you.

An initial brand strategy and positioning exploration for Hayleigh Monique Coaching.

Prepared as a starting point for discussion.

01 / The opportunity

Successful on paper. Searching for something more.

For women who have achieved a lot, but feel disconnected from what they really want.

The brand should make someone feel immediately understood — without promising an overnight transformation. This is about reflection, clarity and the confidence to choose a next chapter that feels genuinely theirs.

02 / Audience

Capable. Thoughtful. Established.

Professionally established women who appear successful externally, but want greater clarity and confidence about what matters to them.

What she feels

Professionally successful but unsure what comes next. Pulled between her own ambitions and other people's expectations.

What she needs

Space to think, a non-judgemental sounding board and practical support to make considered decisions.

Illustrative audience personas

Three reasons she might seek coaching.

These are working hypotheses based on Hayleigh's questionnaire, not validated research or real customer profiles. Names, ages, lifestyles, portraits and brand affinities are illustrative assumptions for discussion, not verified customer data.

PERSONA 01Success without fulfilment

The High Achiever

Charlotte, 38 · Senior marketing director · London

“I've worked so hard to get here. Why do I still feel like something's missing?”

Who she is
Ambitious, articulate and highly organised. Charlotte is the person friends turn to for advice. She likes being capable and in control, but rarely makes time to ask what she wants for herself.
Life outside work
A busy calendar, long lunches with friends, Pilates when she can fit it in, weekends away and a well-considered home. She values quality over quantity and is happy to pay more for things that feel worthwhile.
What she values
Independence, achievement, good taste and meaningful relationships.
What's underneath
Her career and lifestyle look successful, but the next promotion no longer feels like an exciting answer.
What might prompt coaching
A milestone birthday, burnout scare or promotion that feels unexpectedly flat.
What she needs from Hayleigh
Permission to rethink her definition of success without feeling ungrateful or reckless.
Lifestyle brands she may know
Jo Malone London · ME+EM · Aesop · Soho House
PERSONA 02Considering a different chapter

The Crossroads Professional

Sophie, 43 · HR business partner · Surrey

“I know I want a change. I just can't work out what that change should be.”

Who she is
Thoughtful, curious and pragmatic. Sophie is confident making decisions for other people, but hesitant when the decision could change her own life. She researches every option and can get stuck weighing up the risks.
Life outside work
Balances a demanding job with family life. Enjoys good restaurants, city breaks, a Sunday walk and browsing interiors. She listens to podcasts about careers, wellbeing and personal growth.
What she values
Stability, family, freedom and making the most of her time.
What's underneath
She has outgrown parts of her current routine, but worries that changing direction could put what she has built at risk.
What might prompt coaching
A restructure at work, children becoming more independent or the realisation that she's been saying 'maybe next year' for too long.
What she needs from Hayleigh
A sounding board to separate fear from genuine priorities and turn possibilities into a manageable plan.
Lifestyle brands she may know
The White Company · Reiss · Neom · John Lewis
PERSONA 03Reconnecting with herself

The People Pleaser

Amelia, 35 · Account director · Manchester

“I'm so used to being who everyone needs me to be, I'm not sure what I want anymore.”

Who she is
Warm, socially aware and dependable. Amelia remembers birthdays, checks in on everyone and is often the one who keeps things running. She's quietly ambitious, but tends to downplay her own needs to avoid disappointing people.
Life outside work
Has an active social circle and a full diary. Loves hosting, wellness weekends, travel, thoughtful gifts and finding little rituals that make everyday life feel better. She spends on experiences and personal care.
What she values
Connection, kindness, belonging and feeling authentic.
What's underneath
She says yes too often, compares her life to others and struggles to recognise which goals are truly hers.
What might prompt coaching
Feeling emotionally drained, a relationship change or noticing resentment building beneath an outwardly happy life.
What she needs from Hayleigh
A non-judgemental space to explore boundaries, self-worth and what a more self-directed life could look like.
Lifestyle brands she may know
Jo Malone London · Anthropologie · Lululemon · Space NK

Calm, but motivating.

WarmRelatableHonestModernUpliftingReassuring

Avoid: corporate, clinical, overly spiritual, generic and overly bold visual language.

01 / Mission statement

Why the business exists

To help women who feel lost despite their success reconnect with themselves, discover what they truly want and move forward with confidence.

02 / Tagline

03 / Value proposition

What clients gain, and why they might choose Hayleigh

Personalised, judgement-free coaching for professionally successful women who feel ready for something different. Through thoughtful questions, honest reflection and practical guidance, Hayleigh helps you untangle what's holding you back and find a next step that feels like yours.

05 / Positioning

A thoughtful conversation with someone who understands.

Position Hayleigh as a relatable, supportive coach for professionally established women navigating uncertainty about what they want next.

Her lived experience and non-judgemental approach are the strongest differentiators. The focus is clarity and self-understanding, not instant transformation.
