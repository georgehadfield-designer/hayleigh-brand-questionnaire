Brand strategy exploration · October 2026

Finding your way back to you.

An initial brand strategy and positioning exploration for Hayleigh Monique Coaching.

Prepared as a starting point for discussion.

01 / The opportunity

Successful on paper. Searching for something more.

For women who have achieved a lot, but feel disconnected from what they really want.

The brand should make someone feel immediately understood — without promising an overnight transformation. This is about reflection, clarity and the confidence to choose a next chapter that feels genuinely theirs.

02 / Audience

Capable. Thoughtful. Established.

Professionally established women who appear successful externally, but want greater clarity and confidence about what matters to them.

What she feels

Professionally successful but unsure what comes next. Pulled between her own ambitions and other people's expectations.

What she needs

Space to think, a non-judgemental sounding board and practical support to make considered decisions.

Illustrative audience personas

Three reasons she might seek coaching.

These are working hypotheses based on Hayleigh's questionnaire, not validated research or real customer profiles. Portraits are illustrative, and the brand examples are suggested familiarity cues rather than verified preferences.

PERSONA 01Success without fulfilment

The High Achiever

“I've done everything I thought I should. Why doesn't it feel enough?”

Situation
Established in a demanding corporate role, with visible career achievements.
Frustration
Feels disconnected from her own sense of purpose despite external success.
Goal
Understand what fulfilment means to her now, beyond job titles and milestones.
Coaching need
Reflective space to challenge assumptions and identify her own priorities.
Brands she may know
LinkedIn · Apple · Headspace
PERSONA 02Uncertainty about change

The Crossroads Professional

“I know something needs to change, but I don't know what.”

Situation
Considering a new role, a different career path or a wider life change.
Frustration
Overthinks the options and worries about making the wrong decision.
Goal
Make a considered decision and move forward with greater confidence.
Coaching need
Guided exploration to untangle competing thoughts and clarify next steps.
Brands she may know
Airbnb · Spotify · Monzo
PERSONA 03External expectations

The People Pleaser

“I'm good at showing up for everyone else. What do I actually want?”

Situation
Reliable and capable at work, often prioritising other people's needs.
Frustration
Finds it difficult to separate personal ambitions from expectations.
Goal
Reconnect with her values and make decisions that feel authentic.
Coaching need
A non-judgemental space to explore boundaries, confidence and self-understanding.
Brands she may know
Nike · Pinterest · Calm
03 / Brand personality

Calm, but motivating.

WarmRelatableHonestModernUpliftingReassuring

Aspirational without being intimidating. Conversational without feeling casual or unconsidered.

Avoid: corporate, clinical, overly spiritual, generic and overly bold visual language.

06 / Positioning

A thoughtful conversation with someone who understands.

Position Hayleigh as a relatable, supportive coach for professionally established women navigating uncertainty about what they want next.

Her lived experience and non-judgemental approach are the strongest differentiators. The focus is clarity and self-understanding, not instant transformation.
